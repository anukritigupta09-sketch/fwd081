ABOUT THE LABORATORY

What is DSS?

Distributed Systems & Services

The Distributed Systems and Services (DSS) laboratory at South Asian University focuses on the theoretical and practical challenges involved in building modern distributed systems.

The laboratory explores how autonomous systems such as Internet-of-Things devices, edge and cloud platforms, blockchain networks and software-defined networks can work together to provide reliable, scalable and intelligent services.

The research combines networking, computing and emerging technologies to address real-world challenges involving performance, scalability, security, communication and resource management.

DSS

Connecting Technologies

From smart devices at the edge to powerful cloud platforms, DSS studies how distributed components can communicate and cooperate efficiently.
